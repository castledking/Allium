## Allium custom text. Lines starting with ## are comments and are never shown.
##
## /customtext shows the lines before the first section; /customtext <section> [page] shows a
## section. A line "#vote,voting" starts the section "vote", also reachable as "voting". Sections
## longer than 9 lines get pages with clickable arrows. The file is read again whenever it
## changes, so edits show without a reload. Players need allium.customtext (everyone by default).
## /motd and /rules read motd.txt and rules.txt the same way.
##
## To make a section its own command, add an alias to the server's commands.yml:
##   aliases:
##     vote:
##     - customtext vote $1-
##
## Formatting: &-colour codes, &#RRGGBB hex colours, <gradient:#ff0000:#0000ff>...</gradient>
## and <rainbow>...</rainbow>, as in Allium's messages. Placeholders: {player}, {displayname},
## {world}, {online}, {max} and {server}, plus PlaceholderAPI's %placeholders% when it is installed.
##
## Click and hover events, as the Essentials fork's text files take them:
##   <click:run_command:/spawn>text</click>            runs a command
##   <click:suggest_command:/msg >text</click>         puts text in the chat box
##   <click:open_url:https://example.com>text</click>  opens a link (http and https only)
##   <click:copy_to_clipboard:some text>text</click>   copies text
##   <hover:show_text:'&aShown on hover\n&7Second line'>text</hover>
## Values may be quoted with ' or ", and tags can be nested. A whole line of vanilla JSON text,
## as /tellraw takes it, is sent as it is:
##   {"text":"Click me","color":"gold","clickEvent":{"action":"run_command","value":"/spawn"}}
