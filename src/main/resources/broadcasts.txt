## Allium timed broadcasts. Each #section below is one broadcast, sent to everyone online when its
## turn comes. Turn them on and set the interval, order and minimum players under "broadcasts" in
## config.yml (off by default). /broadcasts lists them and /broadcasts send <name> sends one now.
##
## The layout, formatting, placeholders and click/hover tags are custom.txt's: see the notes at
## the top of that file. Placeholders are filled for each player, so {player} greets everyone by
## name. Lines before the first section are not sent. Edits apply from the next broadcast.
